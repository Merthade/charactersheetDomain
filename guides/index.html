Guides: Level Up in Real Life

Practical answers on making training a game, keeping it going, and tracking the quiet habits around it.

Most plans to get in shape fail the same way. The first two weeks go well, then a busy week comes, and nothing in the plan notices you are gone. A game notices. It keeps score, it pays you today for what you did today, and it has something for you to come back to.

Each guide starts with what works without any app, then the tools already on your iPhone, then how CharacterSheet handles it. Where the honest answer is that a notebook or Apple Health is enough, the guide says so.

Common questions

How do you gamify exercise?

Give every session points for the time you put in, add them up into levels, and reward days in a row. Keep the rules simple, and stop paying for training that has become easy. Read the guide

What stats would a real-life character have?

A short list that covers body and mind. CharacterSheet uses six: Strength, Endurance, Agility, Wisdom, Spirit and Willpower, and your character is only as high as the lowest. Read the guide

How do I keep a workout streak?

Set a minimum that counts on your worst day, like five minutes, and decide in advance what a rest day looks like. When you miss, train the next day. Read the guide

How many daily habits should I track?

Three is a good start. Make each one small and specific, check the list at the same time every evening, and add another only after a few good weeks. Read the guide

How can I track reading time on iPhone?

Time each session with the Clock app and note the minutes, or use a tracker with a timer that keeps the total for you. A Focus keeps the session free of interruptions. Read the guide

Can the iPhone track sleep, water and weight?

Yes. Apple Health holds all three: sleep from an Apple Watch or a sleep schedule, and water and weight typed in or saved by an app. Read the guide
