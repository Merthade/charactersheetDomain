Six stats, one character

Everything you do trains one of them. Each stat goes from level 1 to 60, and every ten levels is a rank.

STR

Strength

Lifting things, including yourself.

Trained by: Weights, functional training, core, HIIT, climbing, boxing

END

Endurance

Going for longer than you wanted to.

Trained by: Running, walking, cycling, swimming, rowing, hiking

AGI

Agility

Moving well, and still touching your toes.

Trained by: Yoga, stretching, pilates, tennis, dance, tai chi

WIS

Wisdom

What you put into your head.

Trained by: Reading, studying, coding, puzzles, languages, writing

SPR

Spirit

The quiet half of training.

Trained by: Meditation, journaling, breathwork, reflection, gratitude, time in nature

WIL

Willpower

Doing the thing you would rather not.

Trained by: Deep work, admin, chores, errands, and your dailies

How it works

From "I should really work out" to XP on the board, in three steps.

1

Pick what you are doing

Tap the plus button and choose an activity: weights, a run, yoga, a book, meditation, deep work. Each one trains one of the six stats.

2

Start the timer

XP comes by the minute while you train. Harder activities pay more, and with an Apple Watch your heart rate counts too.

3

Watch the stat climb

The session lands on its stat's XP bar. Fill the bar and the stat levels up. Come back tomorrow and Momentum adds a bonus on top.

Frequently asked questions

What is CharacterSheet?

An RPG character sheet for your real life. You train, you get XP, your stats level up. Six stats cover lifting, cardio, mobility, learning, calm and getting things done. Learn more

What counts as training?

Weights, running, yoga and the other workouts you would expect, and also reading, studying, meditation, journaling, deep work and chores. There are more than 30 activities, each tied to one of the six stats, and you can add your own. See the six stats

How do I earn XP?

Pick an activity and start the timer. XP comes by the minute, harder activities pay more, and buffs add a bonus on top. Dailies, small things you tick off, earn XP for Willpower. Learn more

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. The iPhone app works by itself. With an Apple Watch you can start sessions from your wrist, and it tracks your heart rate, so more effort earns more XP.

What are buffs?

Bonus XP on everything you earn, given for good habits. Momentum grows 1% for every day in a row you train, up to 50%. Sleeping enough, drinking water and weighing in each add a buff too, and they all add up. Learn more

What happens if I stop training?

A stat you neglect starts losing XP each night, just like in real life. It never drops a level. Miss a day and Momentum goes back by two days instead of resetting. Learn more

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. It reads your workouts, heart rate, sleep, weight and water, and saves your sessions, water and weigh-ins back. Reading and saving each have their own switch in Settings. Learn more

Where is my character stored?

On your iPhone, with a backup in your own iCloud. There is no account to make and no sign-up, and your Apple Health data is never sent to me. Learn more

How much does it cost?

CharacterSheet runs on a subscription, monthly or yearly. New subscribers can start the yearly plan with a free trial. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Learn more

Which languages does it speak?

English and German.

Is there an Android version?

Not at the moment: CharacterSheet is made for iPhone and Apple Watch. On Android? Tap Register your interest at the top of this page, so I know.
